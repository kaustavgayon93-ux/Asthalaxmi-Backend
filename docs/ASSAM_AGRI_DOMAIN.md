## 1. The 6 Agro-Climatic Zones of Assam

Assam is divided into six distinct agro-climatic zones based on rainfall, temperature, soil morphology, and terrain:

| Zone Code | Agro-Climatic Zone | Key Districts Included | Average Annual Rainfall | Soil Type | Primary Agricultural Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LBV** | **Lower Brahmaputra Valley** | Kamrup, Kamrup Metro, Barpeta, Bajali, Nalbari, Bongaigaon, Dhubri, Goalpara, South Salmara, Kokrajhar, Chirang, Baksa, Tamulpur | 1,600 – 2,200 mm | New & old alluvial, sandy silt along chars | Sali & Boro rice, Jute, Mustard, Vegetables, Potato, Daranggiri Banana belt |
| **CBV** | **Central Brahmaputra Valley** | Nagaon, Morigaon, Hojai | 1,500 – 1,800 mm | Alluvial, red loam foothills | Sali & Ahu rice, Jute, Mustard, Sugarcane, Beel wetlands |
| **UBV** | **Upper Brahmaputra Valley** | Jorhat, Golaghat, Sivasagar, Charaideo, Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Majuli | 2,000 – 3,200 mm | Heavy alluvium, acidic loam | Assam Tea gardens, Sali rice, Orange/Mandarin, Ginger, Black pepper |
| **NBP** | **North Bank Plain** | Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Sonitpur, Biswanath, Darrang, Udalguri | 2,200 – 3,500 mm | Coarse alluvium, flash flood prone | Sali rice, Mustard, Black gram, Bhut Jolokia chili, Turmeric |
| **BV** | **Barak Valley** | Cachar, Hailakandi, Karimganj | 2,800 – 4,000 mm | Heavy clay, peaty beel soils | Sali & Boro rice, Tea, Pineapple, Arecanut |
| **HZ** | **Hills Zone** | Karbi Anglong, West Karbi Anglong, Dima Hasao | 1,200 – 1,600 mm | Red loam, lateritic hills, Jhum slopes | Organic Ginger (GI), Turmeric, Maize, Orange, Cotton, Bamboo |

## 2. Complete Catalogue of All 50+ Crops Grown in Assam

### A. Cereals & Grains
1. **Sali Rice (*শালি ধান* - Winter Rice / *Oryza sativa*):**
   * *Season:* Kharif (Monsoon). Sown June–July, harvested Nov–Dec.
   * *Acreage Share:* Over 70% of total rice area.
   * *Baseline Yield:* 2.65 t/ha. Standard moisture: 14.0%.
2. **Ahu Rice (*আহু ধান* - Autumn Rice):**
   * *Season:* Zaid / Pre-Kharif. Sown March–April, harvested June–July.
   * *Baseline Yield:* 1.85 t/ha. High exposure to early flash floods.
3. **Boro Rice (*বৰো ধান* - Summer Rice):**
   * *Season:* Rabi / Summer. Sown Nov–Dec, harvested April–May.
   * *Baseline Yield:* 3.40 t/ha. Highly productive irrigated crop in wetlands (*beels*).
4. **Maize (*মাকৈ* - *Zea mays*):** Grown in hill districts (Karbi Anglong) and riverine chars. Yield: 2.90 t/ha.
5. **Wheat (*ঘেঁহু* - *Triticum aestivum*):** Rabi cereal grown in Lower/Central Assam. Yield: 1.95 t/ha.

### B. Oilseeds
6. **Rapeseed & Mustard / Toria (*সৰিয়হ* - *Brassica campestris*):** Prime winter cash oilseed. Yield: 1.25 t/ha. Moisture: 9.0%.
7. **Yellow Sarson (*হালধীয়া সৰিয়হ*):** High oil content mustard variety. Yield: 1.30 t/ha.
8. **Sesame / Til (*তিল* - *Sesamum indicum*):** Traditional festival & culinary oilseed. Yield: 0.65 t/ha.
9. **Linseed / Tisi (*তিচি* - *Linum usitatissimum*):** Relay/paira crop in paddy fields. Yield: 0.70 t/ha.
10. **Castor (*এৰা গুটি* - *Ricinus communis*):** Host plant for Eri silkworms (*Eri silk*).
11. **Niger (*নাইজাৰ* - *Guizotia abyssinica*):** Drought-hardy oilseed in tribal hill belts.

### C. Pulses (Legumes)
12. **Black Gram / Matikalai (*মাটিমাহ* - *Vigna mungo*):** Post-flood silt crop. Yield: 0.85 t/ha.
13. **Green Gram / Moong (*মগুমাহ* - *Vigna radiata*):** Short-duration summer/kharif pulse. Yield: 0.90 t/ha.
14. **Lentil / Masur (*মচুৰমাহ* - *Lens culinaris*):** Major Rabi pulse in Lower Assam. Yield: 0.95 t/ha.
15. **Field Pea / Matar (*মটৰমাহ* - *Pisum sativum*):** Cool season legume. Yield: 1.10 t/ha.
16. **Pigeon Pea / Arhar (*ৰহৰমাহ* - *Cajanus cajan*):** Bund and upland pulse. Yield: 1.15 t/ha.
17. **Grass Pea / Khesari (*খেচাৰিমাহ* - *Lathyrus sativus*):** Hardiest paira pulse.

### D. Fibre & Commercial Crops
18. **Jute (*মৰাপাট* - *Corchorus capsularis / olitorius*):** Assam is India's 2nd largest producer. Yield: 2.40 t/ha (fibre).
19. **Sugarcane (*কুঁহিয়াৰ* - *Saccharum officinarum*):** Grown for jaggery (*Gur*). Yield: 48.0 t/ha.
20. **Cotton (*কপাহ* - *Gossypium arboreum*):** Traditional hill variety in Karbi Anglong. Yield: 0.60 t/ha.
21. **Mesta (*মেস্তা* - *Hibiscus sabdariffa*):** Jute substitute with high acid soil tolerance.
22. **Ramie (*ৰিহা* - *Boehmeria nivea*):** Strongest natural plant fibre, endemic research in Sorbhog.
23. **Tobacco (*ধঁপাত* - *Nicotiana tabacum*):** Local chewing and bidi tobacco.

### E. Plantation & Cash Crops
24. **Assam Tea (*অসম চাহ* - *Camellia sinensis var. assamica*):** Accounts for >50% of India's tea production. Yield: 2.15 t/ha (made tea).
25. **Arecanut / Tamul (*তামোল* - *Areca catechu*):** Ubiquitous homestead plantation crop. Yield: 1.80 t/ha.
26. **Betel Vine / Paan (*পাণ* - *Piper betle*):** Traditional vine trained on arecanut palms.
27. **Natural Rubber (*ৰবৰ* - *Hevea brasiliensis*):** Rapidly expanding in Goalpara & Karbi Anglong foothills. Yield: 1.45 t/ha.
28. **Bamboo (*বাঁহ* - *Bambusa balcooa / tulda*):** Critical for green economy, bio-refineries, and paper pulp.

### F. Horticulture - Fruits
29. **Assam Lemon / Kaji Nemu (*কাজী নেমু* - *Citrus limon*):** **GI Tagged**. Elongated seedless aromatic lemon. Yield: 18.5 t/ha.
30. **Assam Lemon / Gol Nemu (*গোল নেমু*):** Round juicy indigenous lemon.
31. **Banana / Malbhog (*মালভোগ কল* - *Musa balbisiana*):** Premium dessert banana.
32. **Banana / Jahaji (*জাহাজী কল* - Cavendish group):** High-yielding commercial table banana.
33. **Pineapple / Kew & Queen (*আনাৰস* - *Ananas comosus*):** Concentrated in Cachar & Karbi Anglong. Yield: 21.0 t/ha.
34. **Assam Mandarin / Orange (*কমলাটেঙা* - *Citrus reticulata*):** Sweet orange from Tinsukia & Dima Hasao. Yield: 9.5 t/ha.
35. **Jackfruit (*কঁঠাল* - *Artocarpus heterophyllus*):** Abundant summer homestead fruit. Yield: 15.0 t/ha.
36. **Papaya (*অমিতা* - *Carica papaya*):** Year-round commercial fruit. Yield: 32.0 t/ha.
37. **Guava (*মধুৰিআম*), Litchi (*লেচু*), Mango (*আম*):** Widespread orchard crops.

### G. Horticulture - Vegetables
38. **Potato (*আলু* - *Solanum tuberosum*):** Major riverbank Rabi cash vegetable. Yield: 11.5 t/ha.
39. **Onion (*পিয়াঁজ*), Cabbage (*বন্ধাকবি*), Cauliflower (*ফুলকবি*), Tomato (*বিলাহী*):** Widespread winter vegetables.
40. **Brinjal / Eggplant (*বেঙেনা*), Okra (*ভেণ্ডি*):** Essential warm season vegetables.
41. **Pointed Gourd / Potal (*পটল*), Ridge Gourd / Jhika (*ঝিকা*), Bottle Gourd / Lau (*লাউ*), Pumpkin (*ৰঙালাউ*):** Cucurbits grown on silt flats.

### H. Spices & Condiments
42. **King Chili / Bhut Jolokia (*ভূত জলকীয়া* - *Capsicum chinense*):** **GI Tagged**. World famous ghost pepper (>1,000,000 SHU). Yield: 4.8 t/ha.
43. **Ginger (*আদা* - *Zingiber officinale*):** **GI Tagged (Karbi Anglong Organic Ginger)**. Yield: 8.5 t/ha.
44. **Turmeric / Haldi (*হালধি* - *Curcuma longa*):** High curcumin varieties (Lakadong / local). Yield: 14.0 t/ha.
45. **Black Pepper / Jaluk (*জালুক* - *Piper nigrum*):** Homestead intercrop on arecanut palms. Yield: 1.2 t/ha.
46. **Garlic (*নহৰু*), Green Chili (*কেঁচা জলকীয়া*), Coriander (*ধনীয়া*):** Char area condiment crops.
